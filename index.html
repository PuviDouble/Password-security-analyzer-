NIST Special Publication 800-63B Guidelines

Modern cybersecurity standards have shifted away from complex character substitution rules toward length and entropy.

  • Prioritize Length: Passwords of 14+ characters are exponentially harder to brute-force than short complex passwords.
  • Adopt Passphrases: 4 or 5 random words (e.g. correct-horse-battery-staple) provide huge entropy while remaining human-memorable.
  • No Arbitrary Rotations: Changing passwords every 30-90 days encourages weak predictable variations (e.g. Spring2024! to Summer2024!). Only reset if breached.
  • Check Against Leaks: Always screen new credentials against known breached password databases using k-Anonymity.

Recommended Security Hygiene

Combine strong passwords with defence-in-depth measures:

  • Use a Dedicated Password Manager: Bitwarden, 1Password, or KeePassXC to store unique credentials.
  • Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Prefer FIDO2/WebAuthn hardware keys or TOTP authenticator apps over SMS.
  • Never Reuse Credentials: Unique passwords for every single online account prevent credential stuffing attacks.
  • Audit Accounts Regularly: Periodically run batch audits on saved vault credentials.
